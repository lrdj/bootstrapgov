# Programme stakeholders, 2017–18

The Livestock Information Programme (LIP) ran a stakeholder mapping and engagement exercise during 2017–18. This list is based on that mapping. It is suggested to the current programme as a potential guide to recovering institutional memory: people who were close to traceability policy, systems and industry representation at the time.

Roles and organisations are as recorded at the time of the mapping. Many people will have moved on; the roles themselves (and their successors) might still remain useful entry points.
